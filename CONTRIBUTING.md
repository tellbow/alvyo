# Contributing to Alvyo

Thank you for helping. Bug reports and ideas are welcome as issues
(security issues: `SECURITY.md`).

## Pull requests

- **Contributor License Agreement:** before the first pull request is
  merged, agree to [CLA.md](CLA.md) by writing the sentence at its end in
  the pull request. It lets the app go into the App Store; your work stays
  AGPL-3.0.
- **New data sources, APIs and dependencies:** check their terms first
  (`docs/licensing.md`): what they allow, whether attribution is needed and
  where, and whether their license fits the AGPL-3.0. Name them in the pull
  request, and add them to `NOTICE` and `docs/licensing.md`.
- Setup, tests and checks: `docs/development.md` and the commands in
  `CLAUDE.md`. Every text users see exists in German, English, French and
  Italian.
